The top of Lessons: 6 options

Each phone shows the same moment: HSK 1, Unit 3 “Family and people”, 2 of its 6 lessons done, a 6-day streak and 240 coins. The first phone is how the header looks today, for comparison.

What all six fix:

  • One red thing, not four. Today the level pill, the unit sign, the comic pill and the comic button are all red lacquer, so nothing stands out and the Start button has to compete.
  • One title, not two. “Hello” and “The lion at the gate” sit stacked as two headlines. The comic becomes a small Story button or line.
  • Progress and streak where you start lessons. Showing how far you are and what you would lose is what brings people back. Today none of it is on this page.
