ShiCards

Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

ShiCards works without an account. Then everything you learn stays in your browser on your own device, and nothing about you is sent anywhere.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It keeps your progress safe and lets it follow you to other phones and computers. When you sign in, ShiCards stores:

  • your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google;
  • your progress: your decks, cards, word bank, lessons, streak, coins and settings.

With Google, ShiCards only asks Google for your name, email address and profile picture. It never sees your Google password and gets no access to anything else in your Google account.

Where it is kept

Accounts and progress are stored with Supabase, on servers in Europe. Each account can only read its own progress.

What ShiCards does not do

  • No ads, and no selling or sharing your data with anyone.
  • No tracking across other websites.

Other services the app uses

Typing pinyin sends what you type to Google Input Tools to find the characters. Fonts and some app files load from Google Fonts, Fontshare and jsDelivr. These services see your device's internet address, as any website does.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the menu, tap Your account, then Delete account. Your account and your online progress are deleted for good. The copy on your device stays until you clear it.
