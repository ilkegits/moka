Proven app patterns, built into ShiCards

Layouts and habits that top learning apps use, each with the evidence behind it and a free source you can open. Collected on 18 November 2026 from Duolingo's own blog, RevenueCat, Adapty, Superwall, Calm, Nielsen Norman Group and published studies. No paid libraries were used.

Almost every number comes from a big app or a company that sells app tools. Treat them as a direction, not a promise for a small app.

Onboarding 入门

实

A real lesson before anything else

No account, no sign-up wall. New users learn three characters and trace one in their first minute.

Duolingo moved sign-up behind the first lesson: about +20% daily users.

First Round Review

In the app: onboarding pages 5 and 6
实

You pick the daily goal, nothing pre-picked

Four goals (5, 10, 20, 30 words). The button stays off until you choose and says “Commit to my goal”.

At Duolingo, making people choose and the word “Commit” instead of “Continue” were both wins. Pre-selecting the harder goal lost.

常

Why you're learning, then your level

Six reasons with a character each, then three levels. The level sets where Hanzi of the day starts.

Duolingo uses this order. Only ask what changes the app, and say why.

研

A progress bar that starts fast

The bar jumps on the first steps and slows near the end.

A study of 32 experiments: steady bars didn't help; fast-then-slow bars cut drop-off, slow starts made it worse.

City, University of London

In the app: top of every onboarding page
研

A head start

You finish onboarding with 3 characters in your word bank and day 1 of your streak already done.

Loyalty cards with 2 free stamps were finished 34% vs 19% of the time.

Nunes & Drèze, 2006

In the app: celebration page
实

Ask for the reminder after the first win

Right after the celebration: pick a time and tap “Remind me”, or “Not now”. On phones without reminders, it offers Add to home screen instead.

Calm: people who set a reminder stayed about 3× as often; asking after the first session got 40% to set one.

Hints on the spot, not a slide tour

“Tap the card to turn it over” sits on the first card itself. No swipe-through welcome slides.

NN/g's test: tutorial slides didn't help, and made tasks feel harder.

Streak and habit 习惯

实

Daily goal kept apart from the streak

One round keeps the streak. The goal is extra: a green ring around the flame fills as you practise.

Duolingo split the two: +3.3% day-14 retention, +40% more people on 7-day streaks.

Duolingo blog

In the app: flame on Decks, Streak page, end of round
实

“Streak saved” page

After a freeze covers a missed day, a full page shows the ice-blue flame, the week, and freezes left.

Free freezes were “one of the biggest wins”; two beat one, three taught people to skip.

Lenny's Podcast · Duolingo blog

In the app: replaces the old small message
研

Win a lost streak back, free

“Practise 3 days in a row this week and it comes back.” Three bars fill as you go.

Duolingo's free Earn Back was a “major retention win”. Seven studies: a broken streak hurts less when it can be repaired.

Silverman & Barasch, 2023

In the app: welcome back page, Streak page
常

Welcome back, gently

After 3+ days away: Shi Shi cheering, “Life happens”, and a 5-card warm-up instead of the full pile.

Returning users are about 20% less likely to stick; the first 3–4 days after a lapse are the best time to win them back.

Duolingo blog

In the app: shows once on your first visit back
常

Milestone celebrations

A full page at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 150, 200, 365 days, with a Share button.

Retention jumps most from day 1 to 3 and flattens after day 7, so the early milestones matter most.

Deconstructor of Fun

In the app: shows once per milestone
实

Badges for real skills

Nine gold seals on Profile, each with its own progress bar: words, streaks, writing, stories, HSK 1.

Duolingo's badges: +2.4% daily users. A badge for just signing up did nothing.

Paywall 付费

实

“How your free trial works” timeline

Today: everything unlocks. Day 5: we remind you. Day 7: you pay, cancel any time before.

Blinkist: +23% conversion, −55% complaints. Adapty: +12–36% revenue per user in winning tests. One test won trial starts but lost on retention.

RevenueCat · Adapty · Clearful test

Preview only: Developer › Show paywall layout
实

Yearly first, priced per month

Two plans. Yearly is picked first with “SAVE 50%” and $2.50/month; Monthly below at full price.

Yearly-first layouts: +25–40% revenue per user. Picking yearly first didn't lower sales.

Headline about your own goal

ASL Bloom: +15% trial starts after building around the user's goal.

Close and “Not now” in plain sight

An X in the corner from the first second, and a “Not now” link under the button. The price sits right under the trial wording.

Hidden or delayed close buttons and trial text louder than the price get apps rejected.

Social proof and ratings

“Loved by 1 million learners” and star ratings.

Leagues and leaderboards

Weekly leagues against other learners.

Strong at Duolingo (+17% learning time) but needs accounts and a server. Fake opponents would be a dark pattern.

Countdown timers, guilt-trip copy, hidden close

“Offer ends in 09:59”, “No, I don't want to learn”.

Home-screen widgets and store rating prompts

Duolingo's widget users retain far better.

A web app can't make phone widgets or ask for a store rating. The icon dot and Add to home screen stand in.
